# Interpretation and definitions

GRCr8 RefSeq chromosome accessions NC_086019.1–NC_086040.1 are included; nonchromosomal and mitochondrial sequences are retained during alignment but excluded from the chromosome table. Coordinates in BED files are 0-based, half-open. Coverage is the union of target spans of minimap2 primary PAF alignments; MAPQ >=20 is a conservative view, and all-primary coverage provides context. Target spans include indels and are not exact matched-base counts. Query aligned-base sums are not deduplicated and may exceed chromosome lengths. Potential duplicated regions are reference spans covered by two distinct assembly contigs with primary MAPQ >=20; they are candidates, not confirmed duplications. Secondary alignment coverage is a repeat/ambiguity indicator, limited by minimap2 reporting.

Large unaligned regions are >=100 kb without primary MAPQ >=20 alignment; these can reflect repeats, low mapping confidence, reference gaps, structural variation, divergence, or missing sequence. Haplotype-only regions >=100 kb indicate differential unique coverage, not proven deletions. The assembly size difference can include unaligned sequence, duplications and sequence divergence; chromosome coverage differences alone do not necessarily sum to the total length difference. Examine alignments and repeat/reference gap annotation before biological conclusions.
