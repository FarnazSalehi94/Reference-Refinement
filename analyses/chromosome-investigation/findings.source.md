# Initial chromosome investigation

The previous reference comparison uses minimap2 primary PAF target spans and a MAPQ >=20 threshold. Coordinates in the BED files are zero-based, half-open. These are alignment spans, not base-level identity or proof of absence. Results for Verkko and k-mer evidence are pending.

Hap1 is 2,666,936,900 bp and hap2 is 2,910,952,370 bp, a 244,015,470 bp difference. Relative to hap1, hap2 has 152,853,988 bp more confidently covered X reference and 49,162,941 bp more confidently covered Y reference; together 202,016,929 bp, approximately 82.8% of that length difference. Coverage and assembly length measure different quantities, so this is an approximate comparison, not an additive accounting of the sequence difference. Both the primary assembly and hap2 cover about 99.76% of X and 98.06% / 96.59% of Y, respectively. Hap1 covers only 2.86% of X and 14.44% of Y. This supports a sex-chromosome assignment imbalance between the two assembly outputs, but reference-alignment data alone cannot separate true missing sequence, low-confidence mapping, collapsed repeats, assignment and structural differences.

The major X-aligning hap2 contig is h2tg000009l (156,450,971 bp of reference-span union on X). In the primary assembly, ptg000009l covers 156,527,118 bp of X. No hap1 contig has a comparable X span; its largest is h1tg000045l (991,835 bp). Major Y-aligned hap2 contigs include h2tg000027l (30,853,027 bp) and h2tg000014l (25,785,456 bp); primary ptg000003l spans 56,863,308 bp of Y. Several contigs align to both sex chromosomes, so these observations should be interpreted with the separate secondary/ambiguous alignments and repeats in mind. See contig_chromosome_alignments.tsv; lengths from different contigs can overlap and must not be summed as unique coverage.

For autosomes, hap2 minus hap1 in MAPQ >=20 reference coverage is +759,837 bp on chr4, +713,342 on chr7, +380,454 on chr9, +503,326 on chr18 and +1,671,200 on chr19. Largest hap2-only intervals >=100 kb include chr4:87-509350 and 184175135-184425709, chr7:136452389-137014570, chr9:3129867-3509988, chr18:15583-511979 and chr19:22235973-23907173 (all 0-based half-open). Several intervals lie near chromosome ends. On chr19 the hap2 distinct-contig overlap candidate is about 3.85 Mb versus 1.29 Mb in hap1; this may reflect duplication or mapping ambiguity and requires independent evidence.

The original comparison table and BED files are in ../results/. All original assemblies remain unchanged. No reference-relative gap is labeled as confirmed missing DNA.
